Create a Chilimba Group

Set up your group, agree on contribution arrangements and provide the group's mobile money account details.

30-day free trial: Each group receives its own trial. The platform subscription is separate from group savings. Group information is intended to remain stored when the trial expires, subject to the platform's data policies.

Group Creation Rules

Read these rules before creating your Chilimba group. By agreeing, you confirm that you will help manage the group responsibly.

  1. Honest information: Provide accurate group details and use your account responsibly.
  2. Clear contributions: Make sure members understand the contribution amount and frequency agreed by the group.
  3. Group payment account: Enter the correct mobile money details intended for group transactions. Explain any account changes to members and follow the group's approval process.
  4. Accurate records: Contributions, payments, loans and repayments must be recorded honestly. Do not falsify records or make unauthorised changes.
  5. Loans and assistance: Follow the group's agreed procedures for loan approvals, repayment dates, fees and emergency assistance.
  6. Responsible leadership: Do not misuse group funds or use your position to unfairly benefit yourself or another person.
  7. Member privacy: Use members' personal information only for legitimate group purposes. Do not share it outside the group without permission or another lawful basis.
  8. Invitation codes: Share the group invitation code responsibly. Group membership must follow the platform's joining and approval process.
  9. Subscription: The 30-day trial and any later platform subscription are separate from the group's savings. Subscription charges do not become group contributions.
  10. Member agreement: Group members should agree on how contributions, payouts, disputes and other group decisions will be handled.
